1What they’re actually buying

Their North Star (RFP §2, Demo Guide slide 6)

“Liberty Mutual’s future CMS/DXP should enable the teams that bring digital experiences to life to confidently create, govern, reuse, test, and deliver relevant content at speed and scale, so customers receive consistent, personalized experiences that build trust and support informed decisions.”

The problems they named (RFP §1, Demo Guide slide 7)

  • Publishing depends on engineering. Routine updates, new pages and high-volume changes need developers.
  • Content is fragmented and duplicated across platforms, with no clear source of truth.
  • Little reuse. Components, definitions and disclosures are rebuilt for customer, agent and app experiences.
  • Governance exists but is hard to run day to day.
  • Discovery is disconnected. Search, AI discovery, personalization and analytics aren’t joined up, and AI discoverability matters more every month.

What LM.com already has, and what’s missing (RFP §1)

Already in placeThe gaps
Reusable templates and components, JSON APIs, automated validation, search, personalizationIntuitive business authoring, visual preview, approval workflow, governed publishing

So: don’t sell headless delivery as new. Sell business autonomy with governance on top of what they already run.

Three principles to frame every demo step around

Empower the people closest to the content

Business teams handle routine content without tech dependencies.

Build once, reuse everywhere

Content is an enterprise asset, governed centrally and delivered across channels.

Innovate without compromising trust

Speed, with governance, quality, accessibility, security, legal and compliance intact.

Scope signals that change how you pitch (RFP §3)

  • It’s an enterprise content-platform evaluation, not a website redesign. Public sites, agent and authenticated experiences, customer apps, structured content for applications, and future channels are all in view.
  • They don’t expect the CMS to replace every adjacent tool. They want you to say where the platform is the system of record, where it integrates, and where optional modules are needed. Use those three labels out loud in the demo.
  • Properties: libertymutual.com, agents.libertymutual.com, comparioninsurance.com and the Independent Agent (IA) Portal (two properties). All users are in the US.

  • How the platform scales and evolves: roadmap, implementation approach, composable architecture, AI-enabled experiences.

4Use case 1 · LibertyMutual.com

Demo Guide slide 10

“Trusted Answers, Wherever the Journey Begins”

Narrative: a prospective customer asks a natural-language insurance question. The platform retrieves approved, structured Liberty Mutual content, gives an understandable answer, cites or links to the authoritative source, and offers a useful next step. Meanwhile, the publishing team creates and updates that content through governed self-service tools.

Outcome: once approved and published, the content is available to web pages, site search, enterprise search, AI answer engines and downstream apps, without separate manual publishing.

The flow they describe

StepWhat to show
Conversational searchA customer or agent types a natural-language question and gets relevant results or an approved answer
Found and cited by AIStructured content, schema, metadata and templates make LM content retrievable by external search and AI engines
Auto-indexedThe event, connector, webhook or crawl that pushes new content to enterprise search with product, state and audience metadata
Governed page buildA marketer assembles a page from pre-approved components, previews it, sends it through approval and publishes, with no engineering ticket
Profound → CMSContent created or optimized in Profound enters the CMS and stays subject to workflow and governance
Quick analyticsA marketer gets page visits, clicks and scroll depth without a formal analytics request
Stable content APIThe same approved content goes through a stable, versioned API to a site, portal, search platform or conversational experience

5Use case 2 · Independent Agent Portal

Demo Guide slide 11

“One Relevant Liberty Experience”

Narrative: an independent agent signs in and lands on a dashboard of product and bundling content tailored to their role, state and agency and aligned to current Liberty priorities. A Liberty marketer changes which products are emphasized and the agent’s view updates with no development work. Every portal page shares one brand and design because pages are built from governed templates and components. A content admin then updates an approved disclosure once, and it appears in the IA Portal and on LM.com.

Outcome: the agent sees what to focus on and finds approved content to act on a client opportunity, without searching or waiting for IT.

The three layers they describe

LayerWhat to show
Agent experienceBusiness rules by role, state and agency decide which products and guidance appear. Governed templates keep pages consistent, and a component change propagates safely.
Liberty business experienceA business user changes a priority and the agent view updates with no code or release. The page is previewed as two agents with different roles or agencies before publishing.
Shared-content foundationA governed disclosure updated once appears in the IA Portal and on LM.com. Show the approval step, the audit trail and a rollback for that update.

11Rules and things not to say

All contact through the Sourcing Manager on the Ariba message board (§9).

Share only with employees who need to know, “to the minimal extent required to prepare and submit a response” (§10). Check with Matt that sharing with Slalom is covered.

RFPNo publicity

Liberty Mutual’s name and logo can’t be used for advertising, trade display or commercial purposes. Keep demo assets out of reusable demo libraries.

The NPPI clause covers non-public personal customer data. Use synthetic data only.
