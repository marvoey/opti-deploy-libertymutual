1What the real files added

Reading the actual Demo Guide, rather than the text I could extract earlier, changed four things.

  • Liberty’s own agenda is 25 / 50 / 15 minutes (slide 4). Intro and case studies 25, live demo 45 plus 5 Q&A, roadmap 10 plus 5 wrap-up. RFP §7 names the five blocks; this slide gives the minutes, so plan against it.
  • Every use case needs three things (slide 9): the step-by-step screens, the configuration and data controls behind them, and current versus future AI. That’s why each step below has a “behind the scenes” peek and a label.
  • “User choice and suppression” appears on slide 9 next to “media & entertainment” wording. It looks like leftover template text, but the Data Protection Officer is in the room, so the flow includes a short consent moment (use case 2, step 1).
  • The Demo Guide is Slalom-branded. Slalom may have written it for Liberty. Find out whether Slalom is our implementation partner, Liberty’s advisor, or both before you share demo plans with them.

The questionnaire has the same 205 question references as the SharePoint copy, with no hidden sheets or comments, so nothing changed there.

Don’t demo 17 features. Use one synthetic coverage topic, auto rental reimbursement, and let the audience watch it travel all the way through. It makes 45 minutes feel like one story, and it keeps your content model small enough to build in a couple of days.
